Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy explains how Auxanite LLC (“Auxanite”) handles information on this website, auxanite.com, and in the apps and products it publishes.

This website

  • This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and it loads nothing from third parties.
  • It has no accounts and no forms.
  • Like any website, it is delivered by a hosting provider whose servers may keep standard, short-lived logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) for security and reliability.

Apps and products

Each app published by Auxanite has its own privacy details, describing exactly what that app collects and why. You can find them on the app’s App Store listing and on its page on this site. Where an app’s own policy differs from this one, the app’s policy applies to that app.

Purchases and subscriptions made through the App Store are processed by Apple. Auxanite does not receive your payment card details.

When you contact us

If you email Auxanite, your email address and the contents of your message are used to reply to you and to resolve your request. They are not sold or used for marketing.

Sharing

Auxanite does not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers that help operate the website and products (for example, hosting and email), or when required by law.

Your choices

You can ask what information Auxanite holds about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted, by emailing support@auxanite.com.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and Auxanite does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
