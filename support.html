Support

How can we help?

Support for every app and product published by Auxanite LLC.

Email support

Write to support@auxanite.com. To get a useful answer faster, please include:

  • The name of the app and its version (shown on its App Store page or in its settings)
  • Your device model and iOS version
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead

Email support

Purchases, subscriptions and refunds

Purchases made through the App Store are processed by Apple, not by Auxanite, so billing changes have to be made with Apple directly.

  • Manage or cancel a subscription: on your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Request a refund: sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase.
  • Restore a purchase: use the Restore Purchases option inside the app while signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Privacy and your data

See the Privacy Policy. To ask about, correct or delete data connected to you, email support@auxanite.com.

Reporting a security issue

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in an Auxanite product, please email support@auxanite.com with the details before disclosing it publicly.
