aux·an·ite  /ˈɔːk.sə.naɪt/  noun

A material that makes things grow.

Auxanite LLC is an independent software studio. It builds and publishes a small number of apps and products, each made to be useful enough to be worth paying for.

From Greek αὐξάνω (auxánō), “to grow, to increase” + -ite, the suffix of minerals.
